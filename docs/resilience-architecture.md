# Resilience Architecture

A water utility resilience system should connect operational status, customer impact, field activity, infrastructure context, and decision history.

## Core Layers

1. Asset and service area registry
2. Incident and disruption intake
3. Field status updates
4. Advisory review workflow
5. Continuity planning records
6. Public communication records
7. Dashboard and reporting layer
8. Audit and evidence layer

## Key Design Principle

A resilience platform should help a utility understand what happened, where it happened, who is affected, what action was taken, and what evidence supports the decision.

## Example Workflow

1. Disruption reported
2. Service area identified
3. Operational status assigned
4. Field update recorded
5. Advisory need reviewed
6. Public message approved
7. Restoration status tracked
8. Final record archived
